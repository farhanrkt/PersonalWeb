Scope: the home page (`app/page.tsx`). Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

**Recomposed as the cabinet's index card catalogue.** Home orients and routes;
it no longer displays the collection. The top specimen sheet (identity,
determination label, three checkable facts) stays full size and unchanged.
Beneath it, five drawer cards on the board: numeral, name, one line of what the
drawer holds, and a count — `3 specimens`, `8 accessions`, `1 type specimen`,
`1 note`, `contact`. The count is what makes an index honest: it states the size
of what it indexes.

**Nothing from a drawer is reproduced on home.** A duplicate is the failure mode
this structure exists to prevent.

Anti-goals: no availability language, no phone number, no awards beyond ICICyTA,
no second type band, nothing rounded, no shadows.
