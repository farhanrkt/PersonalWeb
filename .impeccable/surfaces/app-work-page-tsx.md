Scope: the four drawers — `/work`, `/research`, `/writing` (+ `/writing/[slug]`)
and `/about`. Visitor mode: **Read** — the visitor has already chosen to look
properly, so structure for comprehension over persuasion.

Each drawer must be **complete on its own subject**, because links from a CV or
LinkedIn land directly in one. None may repeat the home page.

- **`/work`** — the three mounted specimens at full size, then the accession
  register, then a closing sheet home never shows: *what the collection excludes
  and why*, naming the four local-only projects and the five unfinished repos.
  That exclusion sheet is the strongest thing on the page and belongs only here.
- **`/research`** — the type specimen expanded: abstract, the three architectures
  compared, awards, Scopus and IEEE, notebooks, and one annotation slip on what a
  single-dataset comparison cannot establish.
- **`/writing`** — pieces indexed as register rows. An article is an essay
  mounted on a sheet at reading measure, with asides set as **annotation slips**
  rather than blockquotes; the world already owns that component.
- **`/about`** — roles, education, ten certifications with credential IDs,
  capabilities, and a determination-label block for the CV with a download
  action. Facts render on the page; the PDF sits alongside for ATS workflows.

Navigation becomes real routes. Contact remains a sheet on home and in the footer.

Ranges: 3 specimens (stable) · 8 accessions, must hold at 5 and 20 · 1 piece at
launch, index must not look broken at one row · 10 certifications · 9 named
exclusions.

Open — must not be invented: which CV export is current.
